About

Bridging strategy and the people who deliver it

Stefania Mascolo is a consultant with ten years of international experience in strategic consulting, capability development and the coordination of organisational change.

She works alongside companies and public administrations to identify the skills that need developing and to build tailored capacity-building programmes, taking care of their design, delivery and the evaluation of results.

She designs and leads corporate training in transversal and managerial skills, in particular negotiation, effective communication, leadership and collaboration. She also facilitates experiential workshops, team-building events and debriefing sessions, using participatory methods such as storytelling, gamification and group exercises to open honest dialogue, recognise and improve individual and team dynamics, and face the challenges of organisational change.

Her approach combines consulting rigour with close attention to relational dynamics. The aim is always the same: translate organisational needs into learning paths and new behavioural habits that people apply in their everyday work.

Works in Italian, English and Spanish

How I work

Trust is the precondition for change

Assessing an organisation means hearing what people don't usually say out loud. That only works when everyone involved knows how the information will be handled.

01

Confidential by default

What emerges in interviews and workshops is handled with discretion. Findings are reported at the level agreed at the outset, never attributed to individuals without consent.

02

Independent and candid

Leadership receives a clear, evidence-based picture, including what is uncomfortable. The focus is on systems and behaviours, not on blame.

03

Built with your people

Solutions are designed together with the teams who will live them, so change is owned and not imposed.

04

Measured results

Every engagement starts with clear objectives and ends with an evaluation of what has actually changed in day-to-day work.
